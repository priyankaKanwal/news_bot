COMPANY PROFILE — NORTHSTAR DIGITAL (FICTIONAL TEST DATA)
Last updated: 2026-10-01

Northstar Digital is a fictional software company headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
It has three departments: Engineering, Finance, and Customer Success.
The company uses INR for payroll and expense reimbursements.

Working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
The standard work location is hybrid: employees work from the office on Tuesday and Thursday.
The remaining weekdays may be worked remotely unless a team-specific policy says otherwise.

The annual leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.
Employees must submit leave requests through the People Portal.
