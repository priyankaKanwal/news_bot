LEAVE POLICY — NORTHSTAR DIGITAL
Policy ID: HR-LV-04
Last updated: 2026-09-15

Annual leave entitlement:
- Employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
- Annual leave is prorated for employees joining during the year.
- Unused annual leave expires on December 31; it does not carry forward.

Sick leave:
- Employees receive 8 sick leave days per calendar year.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive working days.

Request and approval:
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 5 working days before the first day of leave.
- The employee's direct manager is the first approver.
- Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days require both the direct manager and the HR Manager (Sana Khan, E204).
- Emergency leave may be submitted with shorter notice, but the employee must explain the reason.

Project coverage:
- Before approving leave, the manager should confirm that project coverage is available.
- If two people assigned to the same project request overlapping leave, the project manager must confirm coverage before approval.

Important dependency:
- To identify the correct first approver, use the employee's manager ID in the Employee Directory.
- To check project overlap, use the Project Allocation document.
