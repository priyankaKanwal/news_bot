EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY — NORTHSTAR DIGITAL
Policy ID: FIN-EX-02
Last updated: 2026-08-01

Employees may claim approved business expenses within 30 calendar days of the transaction.
A receipt is mandatory for every expense above INR 500.
Meals are reimbursable up to INR 800 per employee per day when travelling for approved business.
Local home-to-office commuting is not reimbursable.
A manager approves claims up to INR 10,000.
Claims above INR 10,000 require manager approval and Finance approval from Nisha Kapoor (E202).
The employee's manager can be identified in the Employee Directory.

Examples:
- INR 650 taxi expense: receipt required.
- INR 450 office stationery expense: receipt is not mandatory under the receipt threshold, but the expense must still be business-related.
- INR 12,500 hotel expense: manager and Finance approval required.
